and cross-round adaptation. It does not implement claim ceilings, evidence
admission or protocol authority. Those remain behind the C-only Evidence Port.

predicted signature, matched-control plan, falsifier and next decision rule; it
is not reduced to a root-control slot. When a required comparison is missing,
the system schedules a future ordinary SearchRound rather than taking a free
execution.
